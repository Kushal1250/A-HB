Reduced motion disables decorative sequences and removes image coverings. Scratch has an immediate accessible reveal; shake, range and trace have direct buttons. Quiet reading opens all chapters. Offscreen/hidden-document animations pause; particles, flowers and fireflies are bounded. Photographs are not distorted.

This inventory describes authored sequences and connected targets. Browser visual verification is separately reported in the verification guide.
