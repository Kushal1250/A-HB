Dear Anu,

On 24 October, I wanted to give you more than a message that disappears between notifications. So here is a little garden: a place where the wishes are yours, the light is gentle, and nothing asks you to be anyone except yourself.

For your birthday, I wish you a life that feels good from the inside. Not only the milestones that look wonderful in photographs, but the ordinary Tuesdays when you feel peaceful, the small decisions you are proud of, and the laughter that arrives before you even notice you needed it.

May you have the courage to begin before everything feels certain. May you find people who listen with care, work that leaves room for living, and dreams that grow at a pace you can enjoy. When a day feels heavy, I hope something simple—a kind word, a familiar song, a quiet moment—helps it feel lighter.

Friendship does not always need a grand speech. Sometimes it is simply wanting life to be gentle with someone, cheering for what matters to them, and wishing them happiness even in the moments we do not get to see. That is the feeling behind this little gift.

I hope the coming year gives you memories you will want to keep: an unexpected adventure, a conversation that lasts longer than planned, a place that becomes a favourite, and plenty of reasons to say, “I am glad this happened.” There are still so many good moments waiting to become your stories.

You do not have to make this year extraordinary every day. I only hope you get to live it fully, kindly, and in a way that feels honestly yours.

Happy Birthday, Anu. May there always be something beautiful still to bloom.

With warm wishes,
KUSHAL PATEL
